## An Overlapping Event Outside the H2 Model

Germany’s last three nuclear power plants closed on 15 April 2023 — [official announcement by Germany’s Federal Ministry for Economic Affairs](https://www.bundeswirtschaftsministerium.de/Redaktion/DE/Pressemitteilungen/2023/04/20230413-deutschland-beendet-das-zeitalter-der-atomkraft.html). This date falls within the 8–14-day window after the OPEC+ announcement on 2 April 2023. The coefficient for this window therefore does not isolate the response to OPEC+.
